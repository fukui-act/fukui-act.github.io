What is polio?

Polio (poliomyelitis) is an infectious disease caused by the poliovirus. It mainly affects children under 5 and can leave the arms or legs paralyzed. There is no cure once a person has it, but it can be prevented with a vaccine.

A vaccine you swallow

In countries where polio still remains, the oral polio vaccine, taken by mouth, is used. Taking it several times builds protection against polio. One dose costs about US$0.15 on average.

We are close to ending polio

99.9%

Thanks to worldwide efforts, polio cases have fallen by this much.

  • Wild poliovirus still spreads in two countries: Afghanistan and Pakistan.
  • As long as the virus remains in even one country, children anywhere in the world are at risk.
  • October 24 is World Polio Day.

Source: Rotary International, “Ending Polio”

What is Rotaract?

Rotaract is a service organization for young people aged 18 and over, sponsored by Rotary International. Alongside their jobs and studies, members take on challenges in their communities and around the world.

Since 1985, Rotary International has been working toward a world without polio. “Act Day” is a day planned by Rotaract and carried out together with Rotary.
