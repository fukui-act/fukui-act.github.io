What is
polio?

This is a story about a sickness called polio, and about the vaccine that keeps everyone safe. There is a quiz at the end!

Start with the quiz
1

Polio is a sickness caused by a virus

Polio is a sickness. It happens when a tiny thing called “poliovirus” gets into the body. It is so small that you cannot see it.

Polio is most likely to make children under 5 years old sick.

2

How does it spread?

The virus grows inside the tummy. Then it leaves the body with poop.

If hands, water or food with the virus on them go into someone’s mouth, that person can catch it.

3

What happens if you get it?

Most people who get it have no problems at all. But for a very few people, their arms or legs can stop moving.

There is still no medicine that can cure polio after you get it.

4

A vaccine can stop it!

But don’t worry. A vaccine can keep you from getting polio.

In countries where polio is still around, children get a vaccine that they swallow. After taking it a few times, it is hard to get polio. One dose costs about 24 yen. That is a little more than two 10-yen coins.

In Japan today, children get the vaccine as a shot when they are babies. Ask someone in your family about it!

5

Polio is almost gone

Long ago, polio was in many countries around the world. In Japan too, many children got polio.

Today, the poliovirus is still found in Afghanistan and Pakistan. That is only

Vaccines for children all over the world

A group of friends called “Rotary” has been helping to bring vaccines to children all over the world for more than 40 years.

Every year, October 24 is “World Polio Day.” On this day, people all over the world ask everyone to help end polio.
